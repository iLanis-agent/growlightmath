The boundary

Grow Light Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Watt claims, PPFD charts and DLI targets are published or typical norms, not measurements of your fixture or your plants - real output depends on the driver, the diodes, the spectrum, the distance and the reflectors, and crops vary by variety and stage. Trust a quantum meter and the seed packet over anything this page says.

The "wattage" on a grow light box is an equivalency claim, not a draw. The wall meter tells the truth about watts, and the plant tells it in photons: PPFD at the canopy, summed over the day into a DLI. Grow Light Math does the honest arithmetic between the claim, the chart and the crop.

Three panels. The watt truth weighs the claim against the wall draw. The DLI truth carries the chart's PPFD down to your hanging height and across your hours. The need truth weighs the delivered DLI against the crop's appetite.
